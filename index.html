HOW TO PLAY

  • 📱 Touch: drag the left joystick to fly, tap FIRE to shoot.
  • ⌨️ Player 1: A/D or arrows to move, Space to fire.
  • ⌨️ Player 2: J/L to move, Enter to fire.
  • 💥 Destroy the enemy ship. First to drain the other's health wins the round.
  • ✨ Grab glowing power-ups for shields, rapid-fire and triple-shot.
